# Artwork study summaries

[Artwork collection](index.html)

Only these summaries are retained. The old candidate SVGs, briefs, manifests,
contact sheets, interactive galleries, and study-only verification scripts were
removed. Approved production artwork remains in `priv/static/`.

## Bottle illustrations

Explored ten directions for each of eight wine types: 80 candidates in total.
Directions included contour drawing, engraving, dry brush, cut-paper, ink wash,
geometric, botanical, linocut, and folk interpretations.

**Decision:** select cut-paper **#05** for every type, with restrained two-tone
shading, inset labels, irregular silhouettes, and simple highlights.

- **Red, white, rosé, orange:** retain the selected artwork.
- **Sparkling:** dark-green champagne glass, long neck, and a seated domed cork.
- **Dessert:** a tall, slender amber half-bottle with a continuous taper and a
  long, neck-width gold capsule; cream label with a diamond emblem.
- **Sake:** ivory flask, blue rim, burgundy label, and a hand-cut 酒 symbol.
- **Other:** neutral olive glass, broader flat shoulders and straight sides to
  distinguish it from orange wine, and a hand-cut question-mark label.

[Compare the current bottles](current-bottles.html). Uploaded wine photos take
precedence over these placeholders.

## Person avatars

Explored ten person-with-wine compositions: sipping, toasting, pouring, swirling,
and enjoying the aroma, across ink-line, editorial, brush, and cut-paper styles.
A simplified silhouette was also tried. Small-size legibility, anatomy, and glass
outlines proved difficult to balance with the bottle artwork.

**Decision:** shelve the custom person avatars. Use the unmodified Spacepixel
Creative Unsplash illustration as the fallback, with uploaded photos taking
precedence. [Current avatar and logo](current-logo.html);
[license and attribution](../../LICENSE.md).

## Animal avatars

Explored twelve cut-paper companions: four cats, four dachshunds, and four
whippet/greyhound interpretations, holding or investigating wine. The first set
was considered too rigid and geometric.

A softer follow-up tried three organic paper compositions: a lounging cat,
a long-nosed dog inspecting wine inside an oversized glass, and a dog balancing
a flute on its head.

**Decision:** shelve all animal avatars. None is used in production.

## UI directions

Compared **Ledger**, **Notebook**, and **Bare** approaches.

**Decision:** Ledger — white background, system typography, compact summaries,
thin rules, and a muted burgundy accent. There is no theme switcher.
[Current UI contracts](../ui.md).

## Sparkling closures

Compared four outline treatments: sealed foil, an exposed mushroom cork, torn
foil around the cork, and an open glass rim.

**Decision:** retain the recognizable seated cork, then redraw it as the approved
dark-green cut-paper champagne bottle without wire-cage clutter.
