# Vivino Export Schema

Three CSV files exported from Vivino.

## cellar.csv
Columns: Winery, Wine name, Vintage, Region, Country, Regional wine style, Average rating, Wine ratings count, Wine type, Link to wine, User cellar count

## full_wine_list.csv
Columns: Winery, Wine name, Vintage, Region, Country, Regional wine style, Average rating, Scan date, Scan/Review Location, Your rating, Your review, Personal Note, Wine type, Drinking Window, Link to wine, Label image

## wishlisted.csv
Columns: Winery, Wine name, Vintage, Region, Country, Regional wine style, Average rating, Scan date, Scan/Review Location, Your rating, Your review, Personal Note, Wine type, Wishlisted date, Drinking Window, Link to wine, Label image
